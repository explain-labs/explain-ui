Each field has a control that matches its type:

- **Numbers** have a spinner or a slider. Type a value, or use the arrows.
- **Switches** turn something on or off, e.g. whether a model is enabled.
- **Lists** choose one option, e.g. a mode or the compartment a connection runs to.
- **Multi-lists** choose several models at once. These usually change the wiring of the model, and some only take full effect after a rebuild.
- **Grey fields** are read-only information.
