- **Drainage** and **return cannula**: the cannula type and size. A smaller cannula has more resistance, so less flow.
- **Drainage** and **return site**: the compartments the cannulas sit in.

The sites decide the configuration:

- **VA ECMO** returns blood to an **artery** (e.g. the aortic arch). It supports both the heart and the lungs.
- **VV ECMO** returns blood to a **vein** or the right atrium. It supports the lungs only.

Changes apply live, and the lines in the diagram move with them.
