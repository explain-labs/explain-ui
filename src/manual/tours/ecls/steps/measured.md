The circuit's measurements, updated about once a second:

- **Flow** (averaged) and **Flow inst** (instantaneous),
- **Pump P**: the pressure the pump generates,
- **P venous**: drainage-line pressure. It can go negative when a centrifugal pump pulls harder than the patient can fill.
- **P internal**: after the pump, before the oxygenator,
- **P arterial**: in the return line, after the oxygenator. The gap between P internal and P arterial is the pressure drop across the oxygenator,
- **SvO₂**: saturation of the blood entering the circuit,
- **post-oxy SO₂** and **pCO₂**: blood leaving the oxygenator.
