Choose the **pump**. These are real devices, each with its own pressure–flow behaviour. The line under the list shows its type, maximum speed and priming volume.

- **Centrifugal pumps** (PediMag, CentriMag, Rotaflow, Bio-Pump): flow depends on the pump speed **and** on the pressures before and after the pump. More resistance or a poorly filled patient means less flow at the same speed, just like the real thing.
- **Roller pump**: a displacement pump. Flow follows the pump speed and is almost independent of pressure.

If the current speed is above the new pump's maximum, it is lowered to that maximum.
