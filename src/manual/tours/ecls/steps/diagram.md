While ECLS is on, the **circuit** appears in the diagram: drainage line, pump, oxygenator and return line. The pump spins with its speed, and the flow dots show blood moving through the circuit. A clamped circuit is drawn without blood flow.

Under the oxygenator runs the **sweep gas**: from SWEEP through the gas side of the oxygenator to EXHAUST, against the direction of the blood. Its dots follow the sweep gas flow and stop at 0 L/min.
