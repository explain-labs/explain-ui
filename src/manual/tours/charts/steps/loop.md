Each heartbeat traces one loop, running counter-clockwise:

- the **bottom edge** is filling (diastole),
- the **right edge** is isovolumetric contraction,
- the **top edge** is ejection,
- the **left edge** is relaxation.

The **width** is the stroke volume and the **area** is the stroke work. More filling (preload) shifts the loop to the right. A higher afterload raises the top edge and narrows the loop. Stronger contractility moves the end-systolic corner (top left) up and to the left, which widens the loop.

> Try **Common tasks → Contractility** or **Systemic vascular resistance (afterload)** and watch how the loop moves.
