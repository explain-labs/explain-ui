Valves, vessels and shunts are the **lines** between compartments. The highlighted lines are the shunts that matter most in neonates (where this patient has them):

- **ductus arteriosus**: between the aorta and the pulmonary artery,
- **foramen ovale**: between the left and right atrium,
- **VSD**: between the left and right ventricle.
