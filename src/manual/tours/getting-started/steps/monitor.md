The patient monitor works like the real thing:

- **ECG** with the heart rate,
- **SpO₂ pre** and **SpO₂ post**: saturation before and after the ductus arteriosus (right arm and leg),
- **ABP**: arterial blood pressure, systolic/diastolic with the mean in brackets.

The third tab shows the **ventilator graphs**: pressure, flow and volume.
