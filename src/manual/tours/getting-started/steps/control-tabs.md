These are the control panels:

- **Model editor**: change any parameter of any part of the model.
- **Common tasks**: one-click nudges such as raising resistance or lowering contractility.
- **Ventilator**, **ECLS** and **Resuscitation**: devices and interventions.
- **Pregnancy / Labor**: maternal and fetal scenarios.
- **Scaler**: scale the patient to another weight.
- **Event scheduler**: bundle changes into a named event and fire it when you choose.

Each panel has its own chapter in the manual.
