Explain is a live simulation of a patient's circulation, breathing and metabolism. Everything you see is computed as you watch.

This tour takes about two minutes. Each step points at one part of the screen.

- **Next** / **Back** (or the arrow keys) move through the steps.
- **Esc** or the **×** closes the tour. You can reopen it from **Help** in the top bar.
- Some steps ask you to *do* something. The tour moves on when you have done it.
