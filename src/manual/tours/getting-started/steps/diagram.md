The diagram shows the circulation as **compartments** (discs) connected by **lines**.

- **Colour** is the oxygen content of the blood: red is oxygen-rich, blue is oxygen-poor.
- **Size** is the volume of blood in a compartment. The heart chambers grow and shrink with every beat.
- **Moving dots** on a line show blood flow. They move in the direction of flow; more dots mean more flow.
