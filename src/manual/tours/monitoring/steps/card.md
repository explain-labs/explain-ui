Each card shows:

- the **label**, the **value** and its **unit**. Two values show as *a/b*, e.g. systolic/diastolic,
- the **min–max** over the trend window,
- a **sparkline** of the recent trend: green while the value is rising, red while it is falling.

**Hover** over a card for details: min, mean, max and the current value, plus the engine path it reads from.
