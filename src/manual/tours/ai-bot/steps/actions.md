When the bot proposes a change, it appears as an **action card** under its reply, with a short description:

- **Apply** carries it out, and **Dismiss** ignores it. With several actions, **Apply all** applies them in order.
- *can't apply* means the action failed a safety check (e.g. a value out of range, or not allowed in this mode). The reason is shown.

Nothing changes in the patient until you click **Apply**, unless auto-apply is on.
