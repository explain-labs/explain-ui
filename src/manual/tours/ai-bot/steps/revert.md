- **Revert** (↶) undoes all live changes to the patient (parameters, tasks, tuning, devices) by reloading it exactly as it was loaded. Your diagram layout, dashboards and events stay.
- **New conversation** (+) clears the chat, so the bot starts without the history.

The bot sends your questions to the Explain bot service. If it isn't configured on this server, you'll see an error under the input box.
