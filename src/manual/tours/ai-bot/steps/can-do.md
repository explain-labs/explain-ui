The bot can:

- **change parameters** and **run model functions**, with the same limits as the model editor,
- **start** and **stop** the simulation,
- create **events**. These are saved in the Event scheduler for you to fire; the bot doesn't fire them,
- **edit the diagram**,
- **tune** the patient towards targets such as MAP, cardiac output, heart rate, SpO₂, pO₂, pCO₂, pH, base excess or blood volume,
- **build a whole new patient** from a description or a file.
