The **inspector** shows the settings of the selected item:

- **models**: the engine model(s) it represents, which drive its animation,
- **opacity**, **z-index** (what is drawn on top) and **O₂ tinting** (colour by oxygen content, or a fixed colour),
- for compartments: **colour**, **picture**, **size** (X/Y), **rotation** and the **caption** (text, size, colour, offset),
- for connections: **path shape** and **width**.

Changes show immediately.
