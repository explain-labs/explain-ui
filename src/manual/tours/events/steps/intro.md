An **event** is a named bundle of changes that you can fire with one click, or at a set moment in the simulation. For example:

- *"Pneumothorax"*: several lung parameters change at once,
- *"Sepsis"*: vascular tone falls and metabolism rises, slowly, over a few minutes,
- a teaching scenario that runs by itself: *at 60 s the saturation drops, at 120 s the duct closes*.

The top box is the **builder**. Saved events are listed below it.
