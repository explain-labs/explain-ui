Use **−** / **+** or type a value. Changes apply immediately.

- **PIP** / **PIP max**: peak inspiratory pressure (cmH₂O),
- **Vt target**: target tidal volume (mL), in PRVC,
- **Rate** (/min) and **Tinsp**, the inspiratory time (s),
- **Insp flow**: the inspiratory (bias) flow (L/min),
- **PEEP** and **FiO₂**: shown in every mode.
