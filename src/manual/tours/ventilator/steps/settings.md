Use **−** / **+** or type a value. Changes apply immediately.

- **PIP** / **PIP max**: peak inspiratory pressure (cmH₂O),
- **Vt target**: target tidal volume (mL), in PRVC,
- **Rate** (/min) and **Tinsp**, the inspiratory time (s),
- **Insp flow**: the maximum inspiratory flow (L/min); in VC, the constant flow,
- **Rise time**: how fast the pressure climbs to its target (s),
- **Pause**: an end-inspiratory hold (s). It adds *Pplat*, *Cstat* and *Raw* to the measured values,
- **PEEP** and **FiO₂**: shown in every mode.
