The **ET tube** adds resistance to every breath. A narrower or longer tube means more resistance.

Set the tube **diameter** and **length** to match the patient. Try a small tube and watch the flow trace and the measured tidal volume.

The tube's own volume is extra dead space, so a longer tube also raises CO₂.

The **leak gap** models gas escaping around an uncuffed tube. A gap of about 0.5–1.25 mm is a small to large leak in a newborn. The measured **Leak** shows the percentage lost.
