What the ventilator measures, updated about once a second:

- **Vt**: the expired tidal volume,
- **MV**: minute volume,
- **Cdyn**: dynamic compliance of the respiratory system,
- **Ppeak**: the peak pressure actually reached,
- **etCO₂**: end-tidal CO₂.

Other values appear when they apply:

- **Pinsp**: the pressure the ventilator is using in PRVC or with Volume guarantee,
- **Pplat**, **Cstat**, **Raw**: plateau pressure, static compliance and airway resistance, with a pause,
- **Leak**: with a leak gap,
- **DCO₂**, **MAP**, **ΔP**: in HFOV.
