Pick a **mode**. Only the settings that belong to that mode are shown.

- **PC** (pressure control): time-cycled breaths at a set peak pressure.
- **PRVC** (pressure-regulated volume control): the ventilator adjusts the pressure, up to *PIP max*, to reach a target tidal volume.
- **PS** (pressure support): the patient triggers each breath, and it ends when inspiratory flow falls below 30% of its peak. If the patient stops breathing, backup breaths are delivered.
- **CPAP**: one continuous pressure (PEEP). The patient breathes spontaneously.
