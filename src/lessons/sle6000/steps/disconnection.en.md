Press **Disconnect the circuit**. The tube has come off the ventilator circuit.

> What happens to the pressure curve and the measured volume? What does the **capnogram** on the monitor do, and how fast? And SpO₂?

The flat capnogram is the fastest sign that no gas reaches the lungs any more: it is there within one or two breaths, long before the SpO₂ falls. That is why etCO₂ is so useful after intubation and during transport.

Press **Reconnect**.
