In the centre is a replica of the **SLE6000**, the ventilator used in many Dutch NICUs. The patient is a term neonate of 3.5 kg, still in **Standby**: intubated, but not yet ventilated.

The screen has a fixed layout:

- **Top:** the current mode and the message bar.
- **Left:** the buttons **Mode**, **Utilities** and **Layout**, and below them *Manual Breath*.
- **Middle:** the waveforms (pressure, flow and volume) and, on the right, the **monitored values** (what the device measures, such as PIP, Vte and MV).
- **Bottom:** the **parameter tiles**, what you set.

Changing a setting always takes two steps: tap a tile, change it with **+ / −**, then press **Confirm**. Nothing changes until you confirm. That is how the real device works too.

> Open **Layout** and look at the options (waveforms, loops, trends, SpO₂). Close it again without confirming.
