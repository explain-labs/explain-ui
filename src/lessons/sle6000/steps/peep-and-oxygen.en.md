**Oxygenation** depends mostly on the **mean airway pressure** (which PEEP raises) and the **O₂ percentage**, not on the minute volume.

1. Raise **O₂** to 40 %. Watch SpO₂ and the **pO₂** in the blood gas.
2. Set O₂ back to 21 % and raise **PEEP** to 8. What happens to the pO₂, and to the tidal volume (PIP − PEEP is now smaller)?

> A healthy lung is already open, so here PEEP has little benefit for oxygen. In the atelectasis step you will see when it does help.

**OxyGenie:** press and hold the **O₂** tile for 2 seconds. The SLE6000 can then adjust the O₂ automatically to keep SpO₂ in a target range. Try it later in free play.
