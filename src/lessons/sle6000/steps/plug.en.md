A **mucus plug** can block a bronchus completely.

Press **Plug in the left bronchus**, and watch the waveforms on the ventilator.

> What happens to the flow curve and the tidal volume? Does the ventilator show a higher pressure? (In pressure-limited ventilation the PIP doesn't change; what you see is less volume.) How does this differ from a tube in the right main bronchus?

Press **Suction** to clear the plug.

Under **Patient → Airway events** you can also plug the right side, or both.
