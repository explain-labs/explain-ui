In pressure-limited ventilation the **tidal volume** follows from the pressure difference (PIP − PEEP) and the compliance of the lungs. **Minute volume** = tidal volume × rate, and minute volume determines **CO₂ removal**.

1. Raise **PIP** to 20 (tap PIP, **+**, **Confirm**). Watch **Vte** in the monitored values, then the **etCO₂** and, a minute later, the **pCO₂** in the blood gas.
2. Set PIP back to 15 and raise the **rate** to 60 instead.

> Which lowers the pCO₂ more? Why does the pH change in the opposite direction? What happens to the blood gas if you lower the rate to 20?

etCO₂ follows within a few breaths; the arterial pCO₂ takes longer, because the whole body's CO₂ store has to change.
