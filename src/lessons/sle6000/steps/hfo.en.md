**High-frequency oscillation (HFO)** works completely differently. The ventilator keeps a constant **mean airway pressure (MAP)** and oscillates around it at 10 Hz (600 times a minute) with a pressure swing **ΔP**. The tidal volumes are tiny, often smaller than the dead space.

- **Oxygenation** is set with **MAP** (and O₂).
- **CO₂ removal** is set with **ΔP**, and with the frequency: in HFO a *lower* frequency removes *more* CO₂, because each oscillation then moves more volume.
