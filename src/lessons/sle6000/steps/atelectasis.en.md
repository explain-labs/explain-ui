The centre now shows the **diagram**, with the lungs highlighted.

Press **Collapse the left lung**. Part of the alveoli collapses (**atelectasis**). Blood still flows past those alveoli but takes up no oxygen: a **shunt** in the lung.

> What happens to SpO₂ and **pO₂**? And to the pCO₂: does it change much? Why is CO₂ less affected than O₂?

Now press **PEEP 10 and PIP 23**. Collapsed alveoli can be **recruited** with a higher pressure: PEEP keeps them open at the end of expiration, and PIP is raised too so the tidal volume stays the same. Recruitment takes time, and is usually partial: give it a few minutes.

> Compare with the PEEP step on a healthy lung. Why does PEEP help here, and not there? Switch to the **Ventilator** tab: has the tidal volume changed?
