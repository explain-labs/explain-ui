Start ventilation with the button below. It sets **CMV** (continuous mandatory ventilation): every breath is given by the ventilator, pressure-limited, at a fixed rate.

| | |
|---|---|
| PIP | 15 mbar |
| PEEP | 5 mbar |
| Rate | 40 /min |
| Ti | 0.35 s |
| O₂ | 21 % |

In this lesson the baby's own breathing is switched off, so every breath you see comes from the ventilator. (In the step about triggered modes it comes back, and you can switch it on and off yourself under **Patient → Ventilation drive**.)

You can also start it yourself: **Mode → CMV → Confirm**.

> Look at the **pressure curve**: a square wave between PEEP and PIP. Now look at the **CO₂ curve** on the monitor on the right: the capnogram. Where in the breath does it rise, and what is the etCO₂?
