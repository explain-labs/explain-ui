Now the problems start. First: **stiff lungs**, as in RDS or lung hypoplasia.

Press **Halve the compliance**. The ventilator gives the same PIP, but stiffer lungs take less volume for the same pressure.

> What happens to **Vte** and **MV**? To the etCO₂ curve? And after a minute or two, to the pCO₂ and pH?

There are two ways to restore the tidal volume: raise the PIP yourself, or let the ventilator do it with **volume targeting (VTV)**. Press **Volume targeting on**. It sets a target volume (VTV) of 30 ml, and the PIP becomes the **upper limit**, here 30. Breath by breath the ventilator now raises the pressure until the target volume is reached. Watch the peak pressure in the monitored values: it stays well below the limit.

> Why does VTV need a PIP limit above the pressure the stiff lungs need? Try it: set the PIP back to 15 with VTV on.

You can also change the compliance in steps under **Patient → Lung mechanics**.
