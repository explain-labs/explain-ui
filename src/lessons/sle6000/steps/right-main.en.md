A tube that has been pushed in **too far** usually ends up in the **right main bronchus**: it runs straighter than the left.

Press **Push the tube in too far**. Only the right lung is now ventilated. The left lung gets no fresh gas; slowly its gas is absorbed and it collapses.

> Look at the diagram, then at the **Ventilator** tab. What happens to the compliance and the tidal volume? To SpO₂ and pO₂? How would you recognise this at the bedside?

Press **Pull the tube back**. The left lung is ventilated again, but collapsed alveoli need time and pressure to open up again.
