Neonatal tubes are **uncuffed**, so some gas always leaks around them.

1. Press **Leak around the tube**. Compare **Vti** (in) and **Vte** (out) in the monitored values, and look at the **leak %**. The flow curve no longer returns to zero at the end of expiration.
2. Press **Tube too small (2.5 mm)**. A narrow tube has a much higher resistance (resistance rises steeply as the radius gets smaller). Look at the flow curve: does the lung still fill and empty within the set Ti and expiratory time?

> Why could a high leak be a problem for triggered modes and for volume targeting? What would you do about a too-small tube?

Press **Fix** to go back to a clean patient.
