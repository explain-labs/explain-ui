Valves, vessels and shunts are the **lines** between compartments in the diagram. The highlighted lines are the shunts that play a leading role in congenital heart disease (where present in this patient):

- **ductus arteriosus**: between the aorta and the pulmonary artery,
- **foramen ovale**: between the left and right atrium,
- **VSD**: between the left and right ventricle.

> Watch the dots. Which way does blood flow through each shunt? A right-to-left shunt adds oxygen-poor blood to the systemic circulation.
