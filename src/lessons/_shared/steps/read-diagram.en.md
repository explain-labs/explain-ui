The diagram shows the circulation as **compartments** (discs) connected by **lines**.

- **Colour** = oxygen content of the blood: red is oxygen-rich, blue is oxygen-poor. Mixed blood still looks fairly blue. Only nearly fully saturated blood turns properly red.
- **Size** = volume of blood in the compartment. Watch the heart chambers: they grow and shrink with every heartbeat.
- **Moving dots** on a line = blood flow. The dots move in the direction of flow. More and larger dots mean more flow. No dots means no flow.
