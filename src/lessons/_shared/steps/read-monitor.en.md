The **monitor** works like a bedside monitor:

- **ECG**, with the heart rate.
- **SpO₂ pre**: the **pre-ductal** saturation, measured in the ascending aorta, before the duct branches off. Clinically you measure it on the right arm.
- **SpO₂ post**: the **post-ductal** saturation, measured in the descending aorta, after the duct. Clinically you measure it on a leg.
- **ABP**: arterial blood pressure (post-ductal): systolic/diastolic, with the mean in brackets.

The shape of the saturation curves is illustrative. The number is the saturation. A post-ductal saturation lower than the pre-ductal one points to right-to-left shunting across the duct.

Below the monitor are **flows** in ml/kg/min. For the duct and the foramen ovale, a **positive** number means left-to-right (aorta → pulmonary artery, left → right atrium) and a **negative** number means right-to-left.
