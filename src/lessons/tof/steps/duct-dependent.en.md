In this patient pulmonary blood flow is **duct-dependent**. Without prostaglandin E1 the duct closes in the first days after birth.

Close the duct with the button below, or with the slider under *Interventions*. The model closes it in 50 seconds; in real life it takes hours to days.

> What happens to ductal flow, saturation and blood pressure? Why doesn't flow through the RVOT increase to make up for the loss?

**In the model the circulation holds for now.** The saturation settles at about 40 to 45 %, and the blood pressure stays up because coronary flow rises to keep the heart supplied. The body is still short of oxygen, so lactate builds up slowly. In a real baby this acidosis leads to circulatory failure if the duct stays closed, and that can be fatal. The model doesn't show that failure in the minutes of this lesson.
