Instead of keeping the duct open, the obstruction itself can be relieved: by **balloon dilatation** of the pulmonary valve and RVOT, an **RVOT stent**, or surgery. This step starts from the PGE1 state: duct open, foramen ovale as at the start.

Dilate the RVOT with the first button. The model widens it from about 2 to 4.5 mm effective diameter in 10 seconds. Wait until the saturation has settled, then close the duct with the second button.
