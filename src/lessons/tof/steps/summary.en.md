- In severe tetralogy of Fallot, pulmonary blood flow can be **duct-dependent**.
- Closure of the duct then causes **profound cyanosis**. Prostaglandin E1 keeps the duct open until definitive treatment.
- The degree of RVOT obstruction determines how much blood shunts right-to-left across the VSD.
