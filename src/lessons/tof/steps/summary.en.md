- In severe tetralogy of Fallot, pulmonary blood flow can be **duct-dependent**: closure of the duct causes **profound cyanosis** and, if it lasts, circulatory failure.
- **Prostaglandin E1** keeps the duct open until definitive treatment.
- The **foramen ovale** plays little part: closing it, or making it large, hardly changes the saturation.
- How much blood shunts right-to-left across the VSD is set by the **RVOT obstruction**. Relieving it, here by balloon dilatation, raises pulmonary blood flow and ends the duct dependence.
