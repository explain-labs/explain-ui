Early pulmonary hypertension: pulmonary pressure is raised but still below systemic, the foramen is patent and the duct closed, so pulmonary and systemic flow remain equal (Qp/Qs ≈ 1). The paper matches the transpulmonary gradient and resistance of Jones & Crossland [23] closely in this state; so does this engine.

Note the systemic *diastolic* pressure: the paper's column shows it falling with the pulmonary hypertension, while in this engine it stays near its baseline value. The last step discusses this difference.
