A narrow, closing (1 mm) duct. The pressure difference across it is now large throughout the cycle, so the velocity stays high in diastole and the trace is continuous with low pulsatility — the pattern van Laere et al. describe for a restrictive duct. The paper remarks that the very short early-systolic dip of the target recording is matched by the model.

In this engine the jet velocity of a 1 mm duct stays around 1 m/s, below the recording's peak, because the viscous loss over the duct's length dominates at that diameter; the shape is reproduced, the magnitude is not.
