**What the paper did (§3.1.2).** Bischoff et al. [21] measured left- and right-ventricular output and superior vena cava flow in 45 preterm infants grouped by ductal shunt pattern: no shunt, a bidirectional duct, and a haemodynamically significant left-to-right duct. The paper rescaled the model to that premature infant — blood volume, unstressed volumes, elastances and resistances proportional to weight, with minor additional tuning of arterial resistances — and then created the three states:

- **No PDA**: duct closed.
- **Bidirectional**: a 2.2 mm duct with pulmonary vascular resistance and pulmonary-artery elastance raised until the duct shunts right-to-left for at least 10 % of the cycle.
- **Left-to-right**: the same duct with pulmonary vascular resistance lowered until LVO/RVO reaches the cohort's 1.55.

All three use a small foramen ovale and active circulatory control.
