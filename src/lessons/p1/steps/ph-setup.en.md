**What the paper did (§3.1.3).** Invasive haemodynamic data in neonatal pulmonary hypertension are scarce, so the paper used the representative pressures and flows that Jones & Crossland [23] give for three stages: early pulmonary hypertension, severe pulmonary hypertension without a duct, and severe pulmonary hypertension with a patent duct. Pulmonary hypertension was simulated by **increasing the resistance of the pathways connecting the pulmonary artery branches to the lungs** and by **raising the elastance of the pulmonary artery**.

**What the next three runs do.** They start from the Table 2 baseline (3.3 kg, 24 h) with a patent foramen ovale, raise the resistance of the left and right pulmonary arterioles and the pulmonary-artery elastance, and open the duct for the last state. The table reports pulmonary and systemic flow (Qp, Qs), the transpulmonary gradient (mean PAP − left-atrial pressure), pulmonary vascular resistance and the ventricular, pulmonary and systemic pressures.
