Severe pulmonary hypertension with a patent duct: the duct is opened wide (3 mm), so when pulmonary pressure exceeds aortic pressure part of the right-ventricular output bypasses the lungs through the duct. Qp falls while Qs is maintained, and the post-ductal saturation drops below the pre-ductal one — the differential cyanosis of persistent pulmonary hypertension. The chart shows the ductal velocity turning negative.
