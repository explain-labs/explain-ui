This page accompanies the paper *An integrated model for simulation of neonatal physiology — the cardiovascular system* (Antonius, van Meurs, Westerhof, de Boode; *Pediatric Research*). It explains what the paper describes and lets you **run its validation experiments yourself**, in the same model, in your browser.

The paper validates the cardiovascular model in four experiments:

1. **Baseline haemodynamics** of a term neonate against published normal values (Table 2).
2. **Patent ductus arteriosus** in a preterm infant, against the cohort of Bischoff et al. [21] (Table 3).
3. **Ductal Doppler patterns** against van Laere et al. [22] (Fig. 5).
4. **Pulmonary hypertension** against the reference values of Jones & Crossland [23] (Table 4).

Each experiment has a step with a table: the literature target, the value the paper reports, and the value this engine produces when you press **Run experiment**. A run reloads the patient, applies the settings the paper describes, settles the model and then measures for a short window while you watch the live diagram and monitor.

> The paper's numbers were produced with an earlier implementation of the model. The experiments here were rebuilt from the method and levers the paper describes; where the current engine still differs, the last step says so.
